In Buenavista, about 580,000 books. A result's call number says where the
book sits on the shelves. Its interlibrary loan page lists agreements with
UNAM, UAM, Instituto Mora, ITAM, the Benjamin Franklin library and several
Miguel Hidalgo branches: <https://bibliotecavasconcelos.gob.mx/info_detalle.php?id=51>
